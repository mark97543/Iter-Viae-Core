Download these apps onto your smartphone before leaving home. Set them up on your home Wi-Fi so everything is tested, logged in, and ready to work offline when you are in the air.

- **Google Translate:**
  - Download the free app from the App Store or Google Play.
  - **Critical Step:** Tap your profile or the language settings, find **Thai**, and tap **Download Offline Language**. This allows you to translate street signs, menus, and basic phrases instantly using your phone camera or text input, even when you have zero cell service or Wi-Fi.
- **SingaporeAir App (KrisWorld & In-Flight Wi-Fi):**
  - Download the official **SingaporeAir** app.
  - **Set up a free KrisFlyer Account:** Create a free account on their website or app before the trip, and ensure your KrisFlyer number is linked to your ticket. Having a linked KrisFlyer number unlocks **free, unlimited in-flight Wi-Fi** on the plane for messaging and browsing.
  - You can also use the app to browse the dining menu up to 8 days before departure, check flight status, and link your phone to your seatback screen to use as a remote control.
- **Alaska Airlines App:**
  - The domestic carrier getting you down to LAX.
  - Use this to pull up mobile boarding passes, get real-time gate change alerts, and track your checked bags step-by-step from drop-off to arrival.
- **WhatsApp Messenger:**
  - The primary app we will use for daily family communication while abroad.
  - It handles group texting, photo sharing, and voice/video calling over Wi-Fi and data without using international cellular minutes or SMS fees.
- **Entertainment & Video Streaming Apps (Netflix, Prime Video, YouTube):**
  - Download a handful of familiar comfort movies or TV series to keep on your device.
  - **Download Timing:** Wait to hit "Download" until **1 to 2 days before departure**. Many streaming platforms start a 7-day offline expiration timer or a 48-hour viewing window once a download begins.
- **Music & Audio Apps (Spotify, Apple Music, Audible, Podcasts):**
  - If you enjoy podcasts, audiobooks, or custom music playlists, download complete episodes and albums to your phone's internal storage for offline playback.
  - Long stretches over the ocean are a great time to close your eyes, put on an audiobook, and rest without staring at a bright screen.

### **A Note on Noise-Canceling Headphones**

Investing in a solid pair of **Active Noise-Canceling (ANC) over-ear headphones** is the single best comfort upgrade you can make for a transpacific journey:

- **Engine Drone Fatigue:** The airline hands out basic, lightweight wired earbuds at each seat. While they work fine for audio, they let in constant low-frequency engine hum and cabin rumble. Sitting in that constant background noise for 17 hours causes noticeable mental fatigue and headaches.
- **True Silence to Sleep:** Over-ear ANC headphones (such as Bose QuietComfort, Sony WH-1000XM4/XM5, or Apple AirPods Max) digitally eliminate up to 85–90% of ambient engine noise with the flip of a switch. You can turn on noise cancellation without playing any audio at all just to sleep in quiet peace.
- **Plugging into Seat Screens:** Most ANC headphones include a detachable 3.5mm cable in their travel case. You can plug them directly into Singapore Airlines' seatback entertainment screen to watch in-flight movies with crystal-clear theater sound.
