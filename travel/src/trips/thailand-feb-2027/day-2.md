## Travel To Thailand

Today’s operational rhythm is built entirely around preservation of strength, tactical carb-loading, and a prompt extraction from the hotel. Once we cross the perimeter into LAX, civilian comfort is officially off the table.

### The Day-Sleep & The "Last Supper" Protocol

<b>Total Bunk Rest:</b> Since you executed the 4:00 AM protocol and stayed up half the night, sleep as late into the afternoon as humanly possible. Keep the blackout curtains pinned shut. Do not let daylight talk you out of your rest.

<b>The High-Value Meal:</b> Before we step foot outside the hotel wire, <b>we are sitting down for a real, heavy, sit-down meal.</b> Eat like a condemned man. Once we get to the terminal, any hope of normal, convenient, or reasonably priced food ceases to exist. After 16:00, your dining options become $22 rubber airport sandwiches or whatever heated foil trays the airline slides across your lap at 35,000 feet. Fill the tank now with real fuel.

### Extraction & Staging Timeline

Our plane goes wheels-up at <b>22:05</b>. Missing an international wide-body flight is not like missing a commuter bus; they will not wait, and rebooking across the Pacific is a logistical catastrophe.

- <b>15:30 — Hotel Roll-Out: </b>Bags zipped, room cleared of chargers and medicine, and boots on the ground at the shuttle staging area.
- <b>16:00 — Terminal Infiltration:  </b>Feet on the floor at Tom Bradley International Terminal (TBIT).
- <b>The Gauntlet Ahead: </b>Refer back to the Preparing for Flight and LAX sections for the drill on checking bags, navigating the sea of rolling suitcases, and surviving the TSA screening gauntlet.

> 💡 **LOGISTICS NOTE: The Single Exception to Unit Cohesion**  <br> I have Global Entry. This grants me access to the expedited, significantly less invasive government checkpoint. <br> Military doctrine dictates that we maintain unit integrity at all times. However, standard operating procedure permits tactical abandonment at international arrival gates. While you navigate the labyrinth of despair with 400 other exhausted travelers, I will be outside breathing non-recirculated air. <br> Do not take it personally, but so long and thanks for all the fish.
