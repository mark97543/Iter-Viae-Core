Singapore Airlines provides a top-tier onboard experience to keep you comfortable throughout the 17-hour flight across the Pacific:

- **Seatback Entertainment Screen (KrisWorld):** Every seat has a personal touchscreen loaded with hundreds of on-demand movies (new Hollywood releases and classics), full TV series, documentaries, music albums, and games. You do not need to rely solely on your phone for entertainment.
- **Complimentary Headphones:** The airline provides lightweight headphones at your seat for the entertainment system. If you prefer your own wired headphones or noise-canceling set, you can plug them directly into the standard 3.5mm audio jack at your seat.
- **In-Seat Charging Ports:** You will find a **universal AC power outlet** (fits standard US three-prong plugs without an adapter) and a **USB charging port** directly at or under your seat. Keep your charging cables in your personal item so your phone and tablet stay at 100% the whole trip.
- **Free In-Flight Wi-Fi (KrisFlyer App):** All Singapore Airlines passengers in any cabin class get **free, unlimited in-flight Wi-Fi** for text messaging and light browsing. To activate it in the air, you simply link your free **KrisFlyer frequent flyer number** to your ticket before departure or sign in with the Singapore Airlines app once connected to the onboard network.
- Also to note this flight allows two free checked bags up to 23kg each (52 pounds) 

## Complimentary Meals & Drink Service

- **Transpacific Flights (SQ 037 LAX -> SIN & SQ 038 SIN -> LAX):** You receive **two full hot meals** (a dinner service after takeoff and breakfast/lunch before landing), plus mid-flight snacks (sandwiches, dried fruit, chips, and noodles) available upon request.
- **Short-Hop Flights (SQ 714 SIN -> BKK & SQ 721 BKK -> SIN):** A **single hot meal service** or refreshment is served shortly after takeoff on the 2-hour flight.
- **Types of Meals & Viewing the Menu in Advance:** Every service offers a choice between an International/Western dish (e.g., roast chicken, pasta, or braised beef with potatoes) and an Asian specialty (e.g., Singapore stir-fried chicken with jasmine rice or fish with noodles). Singapore Airlines lets you preview the full in-flight digital menu up to **8 days before departure** on their website or app under "Manage Booking."
- **Special Dietary Pre-Orders:** If anyone has specific dietary restrictions (such as low-sodium, diabetic, vegetarian, or gluten-free), special meals must be selected online at least **24 to 48 hours before departure**.
- **Complimentary Drinks Throughout the Flight:** All beverages are included at no charge—wine, beer (Tiger, Heineken), spirits, fruit juices, sodas, hot tea, and water are available during meal runs or anytime from the galley upon request.

## Setting Up KrisFlyer Accounts & In-Flight Wi-Fi

Singapore Airlines provides **free, unlimited in-flight Wi-Fi** in Economy, but access requires linking an individual KrisFlyer number to each traveler's ticket before the flight.

### **Part 1: Setting Up KrisFlyer for Your Parents (Do at Home)**

Each adult must have their own unique email address and account.

1. **Go to the Registration Page:**
   - Visit[ **singaporeair.com**](https://www.singaporeair.com/) and click **"KrisFlyer" $\rightarrow$ "Join Now"** (or use the SingaporeAir mobile app).
2. **Enter Personal Details Exactly as on Passport:**
   - **Full Legal Name:** Enter First, Middle, and Last Name matching their US Passport exactly.
   - **Date of Birth:** Required for age verification and passenger record matching.
   - **Email Address:** Must be unique for each parent (Singapore Airlines does not allow two accounts under one email).
   - **Create Password:** Set up a secure password.
3. **Save the 10-Digit KrisFlyer Number:**
   - Once submitted, the screen will display their **10-digit KrisFlyer Membership Number** (and email it to them).
   - Write both numbers down or save them in your phone notes.

### **Part 2: Linking Their Numbers to the Flight Booking (Critical Prerequisite)**

Wi-Fi access on board verifies identity by checking that the KrisFlyer number is attached to that specific seat/ticket.

1. Go to **singaporeair.com** and click **"Manage Booking"**.
2. Enter your SQ confirmation code (**FRHE7L**) and your last name.
3. Under **Passenger Details**, locate your dad and mom's names:
   - Select **"Frequent Flyer Programme" $\rightarrow$ Singapore Airlines (KrisFlyer)**.
   - Enter their respective 10-digit membership numbers and click **Save**.
   - *(Note: This can be added anytime up to 90 minutes before flight departure, or at the check-in desk at LAX).*

### **Part 3: Connecting to Free Wi-Fi in the Air**

Wi-Fi turns on automatically once the plane reaches cruising altitude (around 10,000 feet, roughly 20–30 minutes after takeoff).

1. **Put Device in Airplane Mode:****
   **Turn **Airplane Mode ON**, then toggle **Wi-Fi ON**.
2. **Connect to the Onboard Network:****
   **Select the Wi-Fi network named **"KrisWorld"**.
3. **Open the Portal:****
   **The login page usually pops up automatically. If it doesn't, open Safari, Chrome, or any browser and type:
   kw.sq.com
4. **Select Complimentary Access:**
   - Tap **"Complimentary Wi-Fi / KrisFlyer Member"**.
   - Enter your **Last Name** and **Seat Number** (e.g., 56B for Dad, 56C for Mom outbound).
   - Tap **Connect**.
5. **Connection Active:****
   **A confirmation screen will show you are connected for the duration of the flight. This works for WhatsApp text messaging, iMessage, emails, and light web browsing (video streaming and heavy file downloads are restricted by the satellite link).
