This packing strategy is divided into three functional loadouts:

1. **The Tactical Carry-On:** Everything needed to survive if checked bags go missing, plus items legally forbidden in the aircraft belly.
2. **The Stowed Baggage (Checked Luggage):** Tropical clothing, durable field gear, and heavier utility items.
3. **The Contraband & Customs Watchlist:** High-risk items that trigger civil fines, customs confiscation, or legal detention in Southeast Asia.

### Loadout 1: Tactical Carry-On & Personal Item

Pack this under the assumption that your checked bag will be sent to Iceland by mistake. **Never check life-support gear, lithium batteries, or mission-critical documents.**  


[ ] Valid Passport (Must have 6+ months validity past March 2027 and ≥2 blank visa pages)
[ ] Hardcopy Documents (Printed flight confirmation, hotel bookings, vaccine/medical records)
[ ] All Daily Prescription Medications (Must remain in original pharmacy bottles with Rx labels)
[ ] Clothing change (Shirt, shorts, underwear, socks)
[ ] Power Bank / Portable Charger (MUST be in carry-on; checked lithium batteries are illegal)
    * Must be rated under 100Wh (approx. 20,000–26,800 mAh max).
    * Rating label MUST be clearly visible on the battery case.
[ ] Charging Cables & Brick (USB-C / Lightning + dual-voltage USB wall charger)
[ ] Noise-Canceling Earbuds / Earplugs (Essential for 17.5 hours on an Airbus A350)
[ ] Eyeglasses / Contact Lenses & Small Solution Bottle (Under 3.4 oz / 100 ml)
[ ] Light Jacket or Overshirt (Airports and Singapore Airlines cabins run notoriously cold)
[ ] Entertainment


### Loadout 2: Checked Baggage (The Resupply Trunk)

Thailand in February/March is dry season entering the hot season. Typical weather runs 88°F to 98°F (31°C to 37°C) with significant humidity. Heavy denim, thick boots, and heavy cotton will make you miserable.


[ ] Lightweight Breathable Shirts (Linen, synthetic tech-blend, or ultralight cotton)
[ ] Lightweight Trousers / Chinos (1–2 pairs; required for temples and government buildings)
[ ] Breathable Shorts (Comfortable day wear; avoid heavy cargo shorts if possible)
[ ] Breathable Underwear & Moisture-Wicking Socks (Merino wool or athletic synthetics)
[ ] Walking Shoes / Slip-On Sneakers (Must be easy to take on/off; shoes come off at every home and temple)
[ ] Sandals / Slides with Traction (Water-resistant; standard daily footwear)
[ ] Compact Toiletry Kit (Toothbrush, floss, basic grooming gear)

>**Note on Clothes:** We will have acces to laundry 5 days supply is sufficient for your check in. 

### Loadout 3: Customs Watchlist & Strict Prohibitions

Southeast Asian customs authorities and Thai law enforcement do not issue "warnings" for these items. Leave them at home:

| **Restricted Item**                      | **Regulatory Status**      | **Operational Risk**                                         |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Vapes / E-Cigarettes / E-Liquids**     | **STRICTLY ILLEGAL**       | Up to 5–10 years prison or massive fines. Do not bring parts, juice, or hardware. |
| **Codeine, Tramadol, Ambien, ADHD Meds** | **Controlled / Narcotics** | Requires advance Thai FDA permits or doctor certificates; can trigger arrest at border. |
| **CBD / Cannabis Products**              | **DO NOT TRANSPORT**       | Even with domestic legalization shifts, crossing international borders with weed is a severe felony. |
| **Pocket Knives / Multitools**           | **Checked Bags Only**      | Confiscated instantly at TSA; never carry into aircraft cabin. |
| **Drone Gear**                           | **Restricted**             | Unregistered drones are illegal; requires CAAT & NBTC permits prior to flight. |
| **Unlabeled Pill Organizers**            | **High Risk**              | Loose pills in plastic day-boxes can be detained at customs for testing. Keep in original Rx bottles. |

### Temple Dress Code Notice

When entering temples (*Wats*) in Bangkok or rural provinces, clothing must cover:

- **Shoulders:** No tank tops, sleeveless shirts, or bare shoulders (both men and women).
- **Knees:** Shorts must reach past the knees, or wear lightweight pants/sarongs.
- **Footwear:** Slip-on shoes or sandals with backs are ideal, as footwear must be removed before stepping onto temple floorboards.
