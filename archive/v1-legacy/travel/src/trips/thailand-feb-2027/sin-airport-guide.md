## Newark(EWR)
**Terminal Location:** Alaska Airlines flies out of **Terminal B**.

**Drop-Off & Check-In:** Ticketing counters and baggage drop are located on **Level 3**. Arrive at least two hours prior to departure; Terminal B security checkpoints can bottleneck during morning banks.

**Security & Gates:** Alaska typically boards from Concourse B2 (Gates 50–57). Once through the B2 checkpoint, you cannot cross over to the other Terminal B concourses without exiting and clearing TSA again.

**Chow & Amenities:** The B2 satellite has limited grab-and-go food and coffee beyond the security checkpoint. If you want a sit-down meal, eat pre-security on the landside concourse before heading down the checkpoint chute.

## Los Angeles(LAX)

At LAX, we execute a two-carrier transfer across different terminals. Alaska operations hub at **Terminal 6**, while Singapore Airlines stages out of **Tom Bradley International Terminal (TBIT / Terminal B)**.

**Arrival Phase (Terminal 6):** Alaska flights deplane at Terminal 6. Head downstairs to the baggage carousels on the lower arrivals level to collect all checked bags.

**The 35-Hour Staging Base:** We exit Terminal 6 directly to the outer curbside islands to catch our airport hotel shuttle on Century Boulevard.

**Departure Phase (Thursday Night at TBIT):**

- **Singapore Check-In:** Take the shuttle back to LAX and enter **Tom Bradley International (TBIT)** on the **Upper Departures Level**. Singapore Airlines ticket counters occupy Aisles B or C. Check-in desks open 3.5 hours prior to departure

- **Clear Security into the Great Hall:** Proceed through TSA screening into the central atrium. Boarding for Flight SQ 37 typically runs from the main gates (Gates 130–159) or the West Gates satellite via the underground pedestrian tunnel (add 10 minutes if assigned to West Gates 201–225).

## Singapore Changi (SIN)
All Singapore Airlines long-haul arrivals and Southeast Asia departures route seamlessly through Terminals 2 and 3.

**Airside Transit (No Immigration Needed):** You step off Flight SQ 37 directly into the secure international departure concourse (usually Terminal 3). You **do not** clear Singapore customs or immigration to make the Bangkok connection.

**Skytrain Shuttle:** Free automated Skytrain cars run every 3 minutes connecting Terminals 1, 2, and 3 inside the secure perimeter. Follow the overhead "Transfer / Skytrain" signs to your departure gate concourse.

**Gate-Level Security Protocol:** Changi conducts full X-ray and metal detection **at the entrance of each individual departure gate**, not at a central terminal checkpoint. Any water bottles or liquids purchased inside the terminal concourse will be confiscated at the gate holding lounge entrance. Empty bottles can be filled at water fountains located inside the gate lounge after screening.

**The Jewel Reminder:** The indoor waterfall and forest dome are located **landside** (outside passport control). Do not exit through immigration into Singapore unless you want to re-clear outbound passport checks.

## Bangkok (BKK)

Suvarnabhumi is a single, massive concourse building organized strictly by vertical elevation levels.

**Arrival & The Long March (Level 2):** International flights dock along Concourses C, D, E, or F. Follow the blue signs overhead toward **Immigration / Passport Control** in the center of Level 2. Walking distances can exceed 600 meters along moving walkways.

**Immigration & Baggage Claim:** Clear passport inspection, descend the escalators into the baggage claim hall, and locate our flight number on the carousel monitors.

**Customs Exit:** Pass through the **Green Channel ("Nothing to Declare")** into the public arrival hall on Level 2.

**Level Guide:**

- **Level 2 (Arrivals):** Extremely crowded meeting hall with SIM card kiosks (AIS / TrueMove). Coordinate an exact exterior door number (Doors 3 or 4) for private vehicle or family pickup.
- **Level 1 (Ground Transit):** Take the escalators down one floor for metered public taxis. Pull a printed paper slip from the automated kiosks to get your assigned lane number. Never take rides from unauthorized solicitors whispering inside the terminal.
- **Level B (Basement):** Train station for the Airport Rail Link into central Bangkok and currency exchange booths (SuperRich offers the best cash conversion rates).
