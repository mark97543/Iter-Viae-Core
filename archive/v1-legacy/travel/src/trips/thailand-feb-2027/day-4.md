The plane touches down at 07:55 local time. Welcome to Singapore Changi, where our Asia epic officially kicks off.

Pull up the **Singapore Airport Briefing** to refresh your memory on the absolute zero-tolerance rules (no gum, no spitting, keep your wrappers in your pockets).

> 🎋 **TACTICAL BRIEFING: THE SINGAPORE CANING DIRECTIVE**

Take a breath, clear the fog from your eyes, and follow my lead off the jet bridge. We have a solid staging window here to explore the madness, grab some real food that didn't come out of a foil tin, and get ourselves scrubbed clean before the final hop.

The plane is on the ground at BKK. You just stepped off a 2.5-hour hop from Singapore, but you’re carrying 20+ hours of cumulative travel in your spine.

Listen up: **Stay locked together.** BKK is cavernous, loud, overstimulating, and completely chaotic. There are thousands of people moving in twelve different directions speaking twenty different languages. If someone drifts off to look at a duty-free whiskey display or an ATM, you will burn an hour just trying to pinpoint their location. **Nobody stops, nobody turns around, nobody wanders off alone.**

**Step 1: The Long March to Immigration (Level 2)**

Suvarnabhumi is built like a massive glass and steel comb. When you step off the jet bridge onto Floor 2, you are in a long concourse flanked by moving walkways.

- **Look Up:** Follow the blue overhead signs that read **"Immigration" / "Passport Control" / "Baggage Claim"** with arrows pointing straight ahead.
- **Keep Moving:** Do not stop at the first currency exchange counter or restroom on the concourse—the ones right off the gate always have lines. Walk briskly toward central immigration before the big tour groups catch up.
- **Tether Rule:** Form a physical unit. Lead the way, keep your parents right behind you, and watch the flanks.

**Step 2: The Digital Form & Passport Control Checkpoint**

Paper TM6 arrival cards are dead history. In Thailand, all foreign arrivals submit the **Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC)** online before landing.

Have these **in hand** before stepping into the queue:

1. **Passports** (opened to the photo page, free of covers or ticket stubs).
2. **TDAC QR Code** saved as a screenshot on your phone (do not rely on airport Wi-Fi loading it).
3. **Boarding Pass** from your Singapore flight.

**The Drill at the Counter:**

- Step up when called. (Officers will process individuals one at a time, but family members can stage immediately behind the yellow line).
- Look directly into the biometric camera and place your fingers on the digital fingerprint glass when instructed.
- Officers are strictly business—no idle chit-chat, no jokes. Collect your passport, confirm your entry stamp (standard 60-day visa-exempt entry for US passports), and step through.
- **Rally Point:** Immediately after clearing the booths, step 15 feet to the side against the back wall and wait until everyone is stamped and through. **Do not wander ahead to baggage claim alone.**

**Step 3: Baggage Claim Carousel**

- Look at the big monitor right past the immigration booths to confirm the carousel number for your flight.
- Proceed down to the belt. Checked bags from the transpacific leg are finally surfacing here.
- Pull all heavy bags off the belt together, load up the rolling carts, and conduct a full count.

**Step 4: Customs — The Green Channel**

- Follow the signs toward the exit through **Customs**.
- You have nothing to declare, so follow the **Green Channel** ("Nothing to Declare").
- Keep moving in a tight group. Officers do random spot-checks, but you will almost certainly walk straight through into the main arrivals hall.

**Step 5: The Public Arrivals Hall Breach (Level 2)**

When the automatic sliding doors open, you step into the **Public Arrivals Hall**. This is the most chaotic bottleneck in Southeast Asia:

- Hundreds of hotel drivers holding up paper name boards.
- Crowds three people deep, heat hitting your face, and constant shouting over cell phones.
- **The Danger Zone:** This is where people get separated. Keep shoulders tight. Do not start walking forward aimlessly.

**Your Immediate Staging Action:**

- Pull off immediately to the right or left near the wall/railing out of the main walking stream.
- Locate your pre-arranged link-up point or vehicle pickup.
- If someone has to hit a bathroom, **one person goes while the others guard the bags and hold the rally spot.**

Once you clear that sliding door, the flight phase is over. You survived the Pacific, conquered Changi, and made it to Bangkok in one piece. Welcome to Thailand.
